Our Story

18 Years of Engineering Expertise, Built on Trust

Founded in 2007 in Riyadh, Rawasi General Contracting has grown over more than 18 years into one of Saudi Arabia's most trusted names in construction. We combine deep engineering expertise with an uncompromising commitment to safety and quality — delivering everything from residential and commercial buildings to infrastructure and industrial facilities.

Our team of more than 950 engineers, technicians and skilled tradespeople operates from our Riyadh headquarters and branches in Jeddah and Dammam, covering client projects across every region of the Kingdom.

Strict Commitment to Schedule & Budget

We plan precisely and monitor performance continuously to prevent delays.

Safety & Environmental Certification

Compliant with ISO 9001 and ISO 45001 across every site we operate.

Full Cost & Reporting Transparency

Clear, regular reporting — no financial or technical surprises.

FAQ

Questions Our Clients Ask Most

Which regions does Rawasi cover?
We cover every region of Saudi Arabia from our Riyadh headquarters and our Jeddah and Dammam branches.
How long does an initial feasibility study take?
Typically two to four weeks, depending on the project's size and technical complexity.
Do you provide a warranty after handover?
Yes — we offer an execution warranty of up to two years depending on the project, with optional maintenance services afterward.

Yes, we have execution experience with both government entities and private-sector companies.
